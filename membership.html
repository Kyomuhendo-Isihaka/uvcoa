03 / Membership

Your expertise. Our network.

A professional home for eligible professionals, trainees and partners whose work advances vector surveillance, control, medical entomology and public health.

01 / Belong

Stronger when we work together.

Membership is open to eligible professionals, trainees and partners whose work contributes to vector surveillance, vector control, medical entomology or related public-health practice, subject to the Association's constitution and membership requirements.

UVCOA connects professionals across districts, national programmes, universities, research institutions, civil-society organisations and implementing partners.

Before publication: membership categories, fees, eligibility and the approved application process are still to be confirmed by UVCOA.
