01 / About UVCOA

Who we are.

The Uganda Vector Control Officers' Association is a national professional association for people working in vector surveillance, vector control, medical entomology and related public-health fields.

Our members serve communities through district local governments, the Ministry of Health, academic and research institutions, civil-society organisations and implementing partners.

The Association provides a common platform for professional identity, continuous learning, collaboration and advocacy. Uganda's progress against vector-borne diseases depends on a competent, supported and connected workforce at every level of the health system.

02 / Our direction

Strong practice. Shared purpose.

Our vision

A Uganda where communities are protected from vector-borne diseases through strong professional practice, effective surveillance and evidence-based vector control.

Our mission

To unite and strengthen vector control professionals through capacity building, research, advocacy, partnerships and knowledge exchange for improved public health in Uganda.

03 / Our objectives

Turning professional strength into public value.

  • Promote professional standards and ethical practice in vector surveillance and control.
  • Strengthen the knowledge, skills and leadership of members.
  • Advance operational research, innovation and use of evidence.
  • Provide a professional voice on policy, workforce development and vector-control priorities.
  • Build partnerships that improve coordination and programme impact.
  • Create opportunities for mentorship, networking and career development.

05 / Membership

Join UVCOA.

Who can join

Membership is open to eligible professionals, trainees and partners whose work contributes to vector surveillance, vector control, medical entomology or related public-health practice, subject to the Association's constitution and membership requirements.

Member benefits

Professional networking, technical updates, learning opportunities, mentorship, research collaboration, conference participation and a collective voice on matters affecting the profession.
