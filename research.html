02 / Research & innovation

Evidence that moves.

UVCOA promotes research that responds to practical vector-control challenges and improves public-health decision-making. We encourage members to generate, analyse and communicate evidence from routine surveillance, programme implementation and collaborative studies.

01 / Focus areas

Research priorities shaped by practice.

01Vector surveillance, species identification and changes in vector ecology
02Insecticide resistance monitoring and resistance-management strategies
03Malaria vector control: IRS, insecticide-treated nets and larval source management
04Neglected tropical diseases and other vectors of public-health importance
05Digital and artificial-intelligence-enabled surveillance tools
06Community-based surveillance and task-sharing models
07Implementation science, programme quality, feasibility and sustainability
08Climate, environment and emerging vector-borne disease risks
02 / In practice

Tested under real field conditions

Members doing the work.

Members contribute to studies and programmes that test how surveillance and vector-control approaches work in practice.

02b / Field examples

Six areas of active work.

Real programmes, real conditions, real results.

01 / DIGITAL

Evaluation and deployment of VectorCam for AI-enabled mosquito identification and timely entomological data use.

02 / CAPACITY

Train-the-Trainer approaches that cascade practical skills to health assistants and Village Health Teams.

03 / MONITORING

Entomological monitoring before and after indoor residual spraying.

04 / METHODS

Evaluation of collection methods, low-cost trapping technologies and community acceptability.

05 / CONTROL

Larval source management and operational assessment of biological larvicides.

06 / STEWARDSHIP

Insecticide susceptibility testing to guide intervention selection.

03 / Our role

Making good research more useful.

  • 01Link researchers and programmes with experienced field professionals.
  • 02Support multicentre and district-level data collection.
  • 03Promote ethical, high-quality and locally relevant research.
  • 04Strengthen scientific writing, abstract development and dissemination.
  • 05Share findings through conferences, webinars, briefs and this website.
  • 06Encourage translation of findings into policy and programme action.
