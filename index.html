01 / Welcome

Welcome to UVCOA

A connected workforce is Uganda's strongest line of defence.

UVCOA is the professional voice of vector control officers and medical entomology practitioners in Uganda. We connect professionals working at national, district, academic, research and NGOs, creating a platform for learning, collaboration, advocacy and professional growth.

Our work supports stronger surveillance systems, a skilled vector control workforce and timely use of evidence to guide public-health action. With a national network of more than 1,000 vector control and public-health professionals, UVCOA provides a platform through which district experience can inform national learning and national priorities can be translated into local action.
